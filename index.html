ABOUT THE COMPANY

Tilak Ventures Limited

Formerly Tilak Finance Limited

Tilak Ventures Limited was incorporated on 18 August 1980 as Tilak Finance Limited and its name was subsequently changed to Tilak Ventures Limited with effect from 13 October 2015.

The Company’s published financial reporting identifies Commodity Trading and Finance as reporting segments.
