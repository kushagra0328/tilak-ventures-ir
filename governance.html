CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

Management and leadership

Profiles of the Company’s directors and key management personnel.

Mr. Girraj Kishor Agrawal
Director and Chief Executive Officer

He is a Chartered Accountant with extensive experience in taxation, corporate law and merchant banking. He has played a key role in the Company’s development and brings strong leadership and professional expertise to its operations. His experience spans corporate finance activities including ICDs, bill discounting, project finance, hire-purchase finance and investment banking, including the syndication of such services for clients. He has also been associated with providing technical and project consultancy to companies for setting up plants. His vision and leadership have contributed to the Company’s growth, achievement of its objectives and strengthening of its governance practices.

Mrs. Tanu Girraj Kishor Agarwal
Non-Executive Non-Independent Director

She holds a B.Sc. in Information Technology and has been involved in handling the business activities of the group companies for the last 15 years. She is also involved in the routine operations of the companies and brings strong interpersonal and communication skills, together with the knowledge and capabilities required to discharge her responsibilities as a director.
