THE COMPANY

Tilak Ventures Limited

Tilak Ventures Limited was originally incorporated on 18 August 1980 as Tilak Finance Limited. The company’s name was subsequently changed through earlier corporate name changes and became Tilak Ventures Limited with effect from 13 October 2015.
